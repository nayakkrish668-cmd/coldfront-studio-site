The what

A complete replacement of an aging gas furnace and condenser with a variable-speed, cold-climate heat pump and new indoor coil.

The why

The old system was loud, inefficient and struggling every January. The owners wanted lower bills and one system that heated and cooled reliably, even at minus 15.

The how

We began with a load calculation, because the right size matters more than the biggest size.

‍

The old furnace, coil and condenser were removed and the ductwork was sealed and tested before the new system went in.

‍

A cold-climate heat pump, a new indoor air handler and a smart thermostat were installed, commissioned and balanced room by room.

‍

We left the home tidy, registered the warranty and explained every setting before we left.

We love it because
  • We sized the system to the house, not to a rule of thumb
  • The new system cut winter heating costs by around 40 percent
  • It shows what a careful install does for comfort and noise
