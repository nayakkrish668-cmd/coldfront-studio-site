What makes a Ridgeline roof last 25 years, not 12?

Most roofs fail at the details, not the shingles: flashing, valleys, vents and underlayment. We replace what’s worn, seal what leaks and ventilate the attic so heat and moisture can’t cook your roof from below.

Quality materials, correct nailing and a full manufacturer-approved system mean fewer leaks, lower energy bills and a roof that earns its warranty.
