Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ColdFront Studio ("we," "us," "our"), operated by Krish Nayak, collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our missed-call text-back service and related communications, including SMS/text messaging.

Information we collect

When you call or text a phone number operated by ColdFront Studio (including numbers used on behalf of our client businesses), we may collect:

  • Your phone number
  • The content of text messages you send us
  • Basic details you share in conversation, such as your name, business name, or the service you're asking about

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to missed calls with a follow-up text message
  • Carry on a text conversation to understand what you need and connect you with the right business
  • Improve and operate our missed-call recovery service
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS messaging

By calling a phone number operated by ColdFront Studio, you consent to receive a text message reply. Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention and security

We retain message and call logs only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our service and for basic record-keeping. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access or disclosure.
