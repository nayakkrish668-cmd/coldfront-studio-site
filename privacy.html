Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ColdFront Studio ("we," "us," "our"), operated by Krish Nayak, collects, uses, and protects information in connection with customer onboarding, website setup, our missed-call text-back service, and related communications, including SMS/text messaging.

Information we collect

When you call or text a phone number operated by ColdFront Studio (including numbers used on behalf of our client businesses), we may collect:

  • Your phone number
  • The content of text messages you send us
  • Basic details you share in conversation, such as your name, business name, or the service you're asking about

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to missed calls with a follow-up text message
  • Carry on a text conversation to understand what you need and connect you with the right business
  • Improve and operate our missed-call recovery service
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

SMS messaging

When you call a phone number operated by ColdFront Studio, our automated system asks you to press 1 to opt in to receive a follow-up text message if we're unable to answer your call. You are only enrolled to receive messages if you press 1; continuing the call without pressing 1 does not enroll you. Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention and security

We retain message and call logs only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our service and for basic record-keeping. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Customer onboarding and uploaded files

When you complete customer onboarding, we collect the business, contact, phone setup, service area, scheduling, website preferences, approved AI instructions, and optional images or reviews you provide. We also record your authorization, submission reference, and Stripe checkout identifiers needed to verify eligibility and associate your submission with your purchase.

Netlify provides website hosting, server processing, and private storage for these submissions and uploaded images. Google Gmail sends sign-in codes and submission notifications. Stripe verifies checkout and subscription status. We do not collect payment-card details or calendar passwords through this form. Onboarding does not connect Twilio, Claude, or your calendar automatically.

Uploaded images are checked and converted to WebP; embedded image metadata is removed. Files are not published through the onboarding form. Access to saved submissions and file downloads requires an authorized sign-in. Notification emails contain a submission reference and a sign-in link, rather than your answers or attachments.

Draft answers may be saved in your browser tab until submission or until you clear them. Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes. Unsubmitted uploads are eligible for deletion after 48 hours. We retain submitted setup details and approved files while needed to deliver your services and handle reasonable record-keeping needs; contact us to request access, correction, or deletion.

Website request form

If you send a free website mockup request through the form on our website, we receive the business name, trade, phone number, and preferred design you enter, along with the web page you used. Formspree processes the form submission and delivers it to us by email. We use it only to respond to your request, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by contacting us below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your information removed, contact us at hello.coldfrontstudio@gmail.com.
